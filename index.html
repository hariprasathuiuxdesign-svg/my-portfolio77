Houzzz

An all-in-one mobile app that guides first-time home builders from dream to handover: plan a project, get an AI-estimated cost, see the house in 3D, calculate materials, track the budget and find verified professionals.

Problem statement

The problem

Building a first home means juggling unclear budgets, blueprints that are hard to read, workers that are hard to find and trust, and delays from rain, materials and payments, usually across separate people and tools. In my research, all 5 users said they would rather manage everything in one app.

What this meant for the design
01Cost tools and design tools live in separate apps. The calculators have no floor plan or 3D, and the design apps have no real cost estimate.
02Money tracking is open ground. Expense tracking, material price updates and brand cost comparison are offered by none of the five.
03Trust breaks on accuracy and lost work: wrong results, glitchy measurements and designs that disappear after saving.
04Pressure kills the experience: constant ads, upgrade pop-ups and subscriptions with no free trial.

Usability testing

Usability testing means trying the design with real people before it is built. Each person gets a simple task while I watch where they hesitate, slow down or make mistakes. What I find is used to fix problems and make the app easier to use.

👤 Task given to the user

Plan your house and see what it will cost

You are planning a 4 BHK villa in Coimbatore. Start a new project, choose your preferences and find out what it is likely to cost.

  1. On Home, tap New project.
  2. Enter the project name, location, house type, plot area and budget.
  3. Choose BHK, bathrooms and extra rooms in Design preferences.
  4. Review the summary and open the full cost breakdown.

What changed along the way

What I learned during the project, and the changes I made to the design because of it.

What I learned
  • Cost clarity matters most. Unclear budgets and extra expenses came up again and again in the interviews.
  • A 2D blueprint is not enough. Users could not picture the house, so 3D became a core screen.
  • Cost tools and design tools live in separate apps, and none of the five competitors tracks expenses or material prices.
Changes implemented
  • The new-project form moved from a side-by-side layout to one guided column with a step bar.
  • The second box on the sign-in sketch became a Phone OTP option.
  • Expense tracking, material price updates and an AI cost estimate were designed in to fill gaps no competitor covers.
  • One typeface, Sora, and a purple colour ramp now keep every screen consistent.
